# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this Discord application (the **Service**) handles information when people use it in Discord. The Service is operated by the application owner identified in the Discord application profile and Developer Portal.

## Information the Service processes

The Service receives information Discord makes available for an interaction, including Discord user, server, channel, message, and interaction identifiers; display names and avatars used to render the interface; command inputs; messages or attachments intentionally submitted to the Service; and recent channel context when Discord permissions and Message Content access permit it.

Members may deliberately save profile settings such as selected video games, game roles and play style, month/day birthday, timezone, availability, and game-category preferences. Saved settings are keyed to Discord account and server IDs rather than names. Profile fields are private to the member by default unless the member explicitly chooses server sharing.

The Service does not ask members for Discord passwords. The current profile system does not create personality assessments or relationship graphs and does not passively build member profiles from server history. Historical birthday scanning and passive long-term profile collection are disabled.

Operational records may include request timing, usage/cost accounting, errors, subscription/entitlement records when billing is introduced, and security/audit information needed to operate the Service. Secrets and message content should not be written to ordinary diagnostic logs.

## How information is used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide requested bot features, maintain conversation context, save member-selected settings, enforce permissions and resource limits, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, comply with legal obligations, and administer subscriptions or purchases when those features are introduced.

The Service does not sell personal information or use Discord API data to create advertising profiles. Discord message content is not used by the Service operator to train a general-purpose AI model.

## AI and other service providers

When a member requests an AI feature, information needed to answer that request may be sent to the configured AI provider. This can include the member's prompt, relevant recent conversation context, attachments, and member-selected profile fields needed for that response. Different deployments may use different configured providers.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process data needed to run the Service. Discord itself processes information under Discord's own terms and privacy policy. Provider retention and security practices are governed by their applicable terms and settings.

The Service minimizes information sent to providers to what is needed for the requested feature. A private Discord interaction means other server members do not receive that setup response; it does not mean Discord, hosting infrastructure, or an AI provider cannot process the interaction.

## Retention

Saved member settings remain until the member changes or deletes them, the server removes the Service and its data is scheduled for deletion, or retention is otherwise required by law or necessary to resolve security, fraud, billing, or dispute matters.

Ordinary saved chat history is subject to the Service's configured retention period. Temporary in-memory conversation state expires automatically. Backups, if maintained, may persist for a limited recovery period before aging out.

The Service should not retain information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

## Member controls

Members can use the private profile/privacy controls to inspect supported saved settings, export those settings, make shared settings private, and request deletion of attributable Service data for their account in a server. Deletion does not delete the member's original Discord messages, information independently retained by Discord or an AI provider under its own policy, or records that must legally be retained.

If deletion cannot be completed, the Service should report the failure rather than claim deletion succeeded.

## Server removal

When the Service is removed from a server, server-specific customer data that is no longer needed should be deleted according to the Service's retention and backup procedures, subject to legitimate security, billing, dispute, and legal-retention needs.

## Security

The Service uses access controls, least-privilege application permissions where practical, secret storage outside the source repository, bounded inputs and outputs, and persistent storage controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The Service is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's age requirements. It is not designed to knowingly collect information from children below the minimum age permitted by Discord or applicable law.

## Changes

This policy may change as the Service changes or legal/platform requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, when appropriate, providing additional notice.

## Contact

Privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion problems can be raised through the in-app privacy controls. For support problems that cannot be resolved in-app, open a support issue at https://github.com/despot707/chatGPT-discord-bot/issues and do not include passwords, tokens, private message content, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
